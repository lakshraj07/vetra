★ Records & PIMS

Pip files every fax, lab, and referral into the right chart.

Inbound IDEXX/Antech results, faxed referrals, microchip records, discharge letters — Pip parses each one, matches it to the patient, and files it in the PIMS you already use. Abnormal lab values get flagged to the DVM in real time.

  • ★ Works across AVImark, Cornerstone, ezyVet, Provet, IntraVet, Pulse, Hippo
  • ★ IDEXX & Antech parsing with abnormal‑value flagging
  • ★ Discharge summary & referral letter drafting
  • ★ Microchip & vaccine registry sync

★ Refills, reminders & follow‑up

Hazel knows what Buddy needs before Buddy does.

Pre‑fetches chronic refills before they run out. Sends vaccine and wellness reminders by species and lifestyle. Runs the 24‑hour, 72‑hour, and 2‑week post‑visit check‑ins your team never has time for. Routes scripts to Chewy, Vetsource, or the in‑clinic pharmacy.

  • ★ Chronic refill pre‑fetch (NSAIDs, thyroid, seizure, derm)
  • ★ Monthly preventive compliance (HW, flea, tick)
